04

Rule reference — LMPC 2011

The exact checklist the engine runs, so a verdict is never a black box.
R6(1)(a)
Name & address of manufacturer/packer/importerMust be complete enough to identify and locate the responsible party.
R6(1)(b)
Common or generic name of the commodityNot a brand name alone — the actual product category.
R6(1)(c)
Net quantityIn standard metric units: g, kg, ml, l, or count for discrete items.
R6(1)(d)
Month and year of manufacture / packing / importA dedicated date declaration, distinct from any batch/lot code.
R6(1)(e)
Maximum Retail PriceMust state "inclusive of all taxes" alongside the figure.
R6(1)(f)
Consumer care / grievance contactA reachable phone number or email address.
R6(1)(g)
Country of originRequired for imported goods.
Proviso
Best-before / use-by dateRequired for commodities with a defined shelf life (food, cosmetics, pharma).
